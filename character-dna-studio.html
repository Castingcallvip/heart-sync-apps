Step 1 · Onboarding

Every character starts with a strand that never breaks.

Lock a face once — bone structure, skin, eyes, hair, build — and every image you generate after that inherits it automatically. Outfit, pose, light, and mood stay free to change; the person underneath never does.

Both paths converge after this step: same DNA summary card, same reference-candidate selection, same QA consistency check before anything is marked "locked and ready."

Step 3 · Reference selection

Step 4 · Auto-QA consistency check

Does she hold up across settings?

Six images, same Locked Traits + reference, six different outfits / lighting setups pulled from the validated test matrix. Check for drift before you rely on this character.

If anything looks like a different person — especially around the eyes or jawline — regenerate the reference rather than pushing forward with a weak lock.
